PRAAN - ASI validation against observed growth
==============================================================================

Question: does the Absorption Stress Index correspond to how Dhaka
wards actually grew between 2000 and 2020?

Observed data: WorldPop annual population and JRC GHSL built-up
surface, extracted per ward over the same boundaries used for the
ranking.

ASI vs observed population growth 2000-2020
  n = 75   Spearman rho = +0.267 (p = 0.0203)   Pearson r = +0.309
  -> no clear relationship - ASI does not predict growth
ASI vs observed built-up growth 2000-2020
  n = 75   Spearman rho = -0.487 (p = 0.0000)   Pearson r = -0.230
  -> low-stress wards grew faster - the inverse allocation rule is SUPPORTED
Crisis Index vs observed population growth
  n = 75   Spearman rho = +0.207 (p = 0.0744)   Pearson r = +0.207
  -> no clear relationship - ASI does not predict growth

WHY THIS MATTERS FOR THE ALLOCATION RULE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRAAN distributes arrivals as population x (1 - ASI), sending more
arrivals to lower-stress wards. That was an assumption. A negative
rho supports it; a positive rho means it is backwards and should be
inverted; a rho near zero means the ASI carries no information about
growth and arrivals should be allocated on population alone.

Whichever it is, report it. An index that fails its own validation,
reported honestly, is worth more than one that was never tested.
