PRAAN — ASI Weight Sensitivity Analysis
================================================================

SETUP
  Ward set     : 75 city-corporation wards with BBS 2022 records,
                 after dissolving GADM "(Part)" fragments into whole wards
  Formula      : 4-indicator ASI (the one that generates the ranking)
  Base weights : pop 0.35 / builtup 0.25 / flood 0.2 / household size 0.2
  Variation    : +/-10pp per indicator, 5pp steps, renormalised to sum 1
  Combinations : 625

RESULTS
  Highest-ranked ward unchanged   : 623/625 (99.7%)
  Mean top-5 overlap              : 4.2/5
  Mean top-13 overlap             : 10.8/13 (min 8)
  Exact top-13 set reproduced     : 41/625 (6.6%)
  Median worst-case rank shift    : 12 places
