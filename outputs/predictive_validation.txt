PRAAN - predictive validation of the Absorption Stress Index
======================================================================================

Question: does a stress index built from year-2000 inputs predict the
population growth that followed? Present-day density cannot be used to
answer this - a ward is dense today partly BECAUSE it grew, so
correlating the published ASI with 2000-2020 growth is circular.

n = 75 wards. Spearman rho, p in brackets.

  component: population 2000     -0.132 (0.260)  -0.160 (0.172)  -0.174 (0.135)
  component: built-up 2000       -0.073 (0.536)  -0.076 (0.515)  -0.124 (0.290)
  component: flood exposure      -0.206 (0.076)  -0.215 (0.063)  -0.120 (0.307)

VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ASI carries no demonstrated information about where growth went. Allocate arrivals on population alone and say so.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE PUBLISHED RANKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ASI remains a description of absorption stress - density,
built-up saturation, flood exposure and crowding are stressors
whether or not they attract migrants. What this test rules out is
using the ASI to decide WHERE arrivals land. That is now done on
population alone, which assumes nothing the data does not support.
