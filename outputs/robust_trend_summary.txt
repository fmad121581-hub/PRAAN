PRAAN — robust trend statistics
==============================================================================

OLS assumes independent years. Water storage is serially correlated,
so OLS p-values are optimistic. Mann-Kendall is non-parametric and
Sen's slope is outlier-robust; the Hamed-Rao variant corrects the
variance for that serial correlation.
