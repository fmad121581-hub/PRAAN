HOW TO STATE THIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Over the GRACE era (2002-2017) the Barind Tract loses water storage at
-0.90 cm/yr (p = 0.004) and the coastal southwest at -0.57 cm/yr
(p = 0.002), while the northeastern haor basin shows no significant
trend (p = 0.40). Same satellite, same season, same method - the
direction and significance depend on the region, which is what the
challenge asks us to detect.

Do NOT say water is increasing in the northeast. Over 2002-2024 that
slope turns positive (+0.36 cm/yr) but p = 0.12, so the honest
statement is "no significant trend", not "rising".

The collapsed pair is a result, not a failure: Bangladesh is roughly
400 km across and a GRACE mascon resolves about 300 km, so the country
supports about three independent samples, not four. Saying so shows we
know what the instrument can and cannot do.
