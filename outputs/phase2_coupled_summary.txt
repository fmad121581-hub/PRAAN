WHY EPSILON IS SMALL
  D_2026/D_ref = 1.44. A proportional response (EPSILON=1) implies a
  0.52 mobility rate, which is not credible, and any
  EPSILON >= 0.4 saturates the 0.5 cap. Mobility is damped relative to
  water stress by irrigation investment, remittances and adaptation.
