WHY EPSILON IS SMALL
  D_2026/D_ref = 1.58. A proportional response (EPSILON=1) implies a
  0.57 mobility rate, past the 0.5 cap, which binds for any
  EPSILON >= 0.71. Mobility is damped relative to
  water stress by irrigation investment, remittances and adaptation.
