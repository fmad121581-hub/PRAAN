# PRAAN — Early Warning System

**Team Voyagers · BUET Department of Urban & Regional Planning · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**

"Detecting the invisible crisis beneath Bangladesh"

## What is PRAAN?

PRAAN translates NASA GRACE/GRACE-FO groundwater depletion signals into actionable ward-level absorption stress indices for climate-induced migration planning in Dhaka.

## Three-Layer Framework

**Layer 1 — The Signal:** NASA GRACE/GRACE-FO TWS anomalies detect structural groundwater depletion in the Barind Tract (2002–2024)

**Layer 2 — The Pressure:** Literature-anchored mobility scenarios estimate 1.43M–3.58M displaced under sustained stress

**Layer 3 — The City:** 1,078 Dhaka wards scored on Absorption Stress Index and Crisis Index

## Key Finding

Groundwater in the Barind Tract sits ~14 cm below its 2002 baseline and has not recovered in 22 years of satellite observation.
